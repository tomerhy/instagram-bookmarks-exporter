Privacy policy
Before you start

When you press Start capture, this extension reads the saved-post content already visible to you on the page — media URLs, post URLs, usernames, captions, timestamps, like counts and hashtags — and stores it locally in your browser so you can browse and download it later.

It captures only while you have it running. It never requests or stores your Instagram password, cookies, session tokens, or two-factor codes, and it sends nothing to the developer or to any analytics service.

You can delete everything at any time with “Clear all data”.
